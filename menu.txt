-- Breakfast

My all time favorite when it comes to breakfast is... drum roll... a Smoothie Bowl!
Here's an example:

Base:
- frozen mango
- frozen banana
- passion fruit

Toppings:
- banana - sliced into thin slices
- kiwi - cut into small cubes
- chia seeds
- almonds - roughly chopped
- coconut flakes
- almond butter

Instructions:
For the base blend all your frozen fruits. If needed, add some milk, yogurt or water.
Put the base into a bowl and add all your favorite toppings! And done - enjoy your breakfast :)


-- Lunch
For lunch, I usually have something light, balanced and easy to prep.
Here is an example for a great recipe:

Grilled Chicken Wraps 

Ingredients:

2 whole wheat tortillas
1 grilled chicken breast, sliced
2 tbsp hummus
1 cup arugula (or spinach)
½ cup shredded carrots
1 tbsp lemon juice
1 tbsp tahini
Salt & pepper to taste

Instructions:

In a small bowl, mix lemon juice and tahini to make a quick dressing. Season lightly.
Warm the tortillas slightly (microwave or skillet).
Spread 1 tbsp hummus on each tortilla.
Layer in chicken, arugula, and shredded carrots.
Drizzle with lemon-tahini dressing.
Roll up the wraps tightly, slice in half, and serve.
